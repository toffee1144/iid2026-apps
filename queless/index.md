# QueLess

## Support

QueLess lets students pre-order food from school canteen stalls and pick it up with a QR code, so there is no waiting in line. Vendors manage their stall, menu, pickup slots and incoming orders.

### Contact us

Questions, problems with an order or payment, feedback, or want your account and data deleted? Email us at [adityafarel840@gmail.com](mailto:adityafarel840@gmail.com) with "QueLess" in the subject. We reply within 3 business days. Please include the email you registered with and, for order issues, your order code.

You can also open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name. Issues are public, so do not post personal details there.

### Frequently asked questions

**Where is my order?** Open the Orders tab to see its live status and your pickup QR code.

**My order was rejected or cancelled.** Orders paid online are refunded automatically. Cash orders have nothing to refund.

**I forgot my password.** Email us from the address you registered with and we will help you regain access.

**How do I delete my account and data?** Email us from your registered address with the words "delete my account". We will remove your account and order data.

**I run a stall and need help with my menu or payouts.** Email us with your stall name.

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

### What we collect
- Account details: name, email address, optional phone number, school, class year, an optional avatar, and your password (stored only as a salted hash).
- Order data: items, notes, amounts, pickup slots, QR pickup code, order history, ordering streak and referral code.
- Payment records: order and transaction references. Payments are handled by the payment provider; the app does not store your card details.
- Vendors: stall name, description and photos, menu items and photos (from the camera or photo library), and the bank name and account number used for payouts.
- Push notification token for your device.

### Why we collect it
We use this data to place and fulfil orders, process payments and refunds, notify you about your order, and pay out vendors.

### Storage and sharing
Your data is stored on the app server. We do not sell your data. Third-party services receive only what they need: Midtrans (payments), Firebase Cloud Messaging by Google (push notifications) and Fonnte (WhatsApp order messages, which use your phone number if you gave one).

### Retention and deletion
Your data is kept until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion, email [adityafarel840@gmail.com](mailto:adityafarel840@gmail.com) from your registered address with "QueLess" in the subject, or open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

### Children
Children under 13 should not use this app.
