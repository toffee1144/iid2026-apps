# StepSecure

## Support

StepSecure is a companion app for a smart-cane attachment for elderly cane users: it shows live location and safety status from the cane, an activity feed, and lets a caregiver trigger a "simulate a fall" demo or respond to a real fall/SOS alert with a one-tap emergency takeover.

Need help, found a bug, or want your account and data deleted? Open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-25

### What we collect
- Caregiver account details: full name, email address, and your password (stored only as a salted hash).
- Watched person's profile: name, phone number, home address, home area, home location (latitude/longitude), and the paired cane device ID.
- Live cane telemetry: location, fall/alert events, and battery/connectivity status from the connected cane device (currently a simulated demo device in this build, not real hardware).

### Why we collect it
We use this data to show live safety status and location, build the activity/alert feed, and power fall/emergency (SOS) alerts and one-tap call/directions.

### Storage and sharing
Your data is stored on the app server. We do not sell your data. To show street names on the map, location coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service.

### Retention and deletion
Your data is kept until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion, open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

### Children
Children under 13 should not use this app.
