# AsthmaSense

## Support

AsthmaSense is a breathing/asthma risk screening companion for parents, teachers and health workers: you enter a child's profile and health history, answer a short questionnaire, and get a live-analysis screen plus a risk-tier result and history.

Need help, found a bug, or want your account and data deleted? Open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-25

### Medical disclaimer
AsthmaSense is informational only. It is not a diagnosis and is not a substitute for professional medical advice or emergency care. If a child is struggling to breathe, seek medical help immediately.

### What we collect
- Your selected role (healthcare worker, nurse, teacher or parent/guardian) and a display name. Sign-in in this build does not require a password or real account.
- Child profile: name, age, gender, height, weight, patient ID, facility, and health history (family asthma history, allergies, previous respiratory conditions, wheezing, night coughing, smoke exposure).
- Screening questionnaire answers and the results of each screening.
- The "live analysis" screen simulates sensor/breathing readings for this build — it does not access your microphone or record real audio.
- In this build, AsthmaSense has no backend server: everything above stays on your device only and is not uploaded anywhere.

### Why we collect it
This information is used to run the screening questionnaire, drive the live-analysis and risk-tier result, and show a child's history within the app.

### Storage and sharing
We do not sell your data. As above, this build has no server component, so nothing you enter leaves your device.

### Retention and deletion
Because data currently stays on your device, you can remove it by uninstalling the app. A future version with account/server storage would update this page. You can also open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

### Children
This app is used by adults (parents, teachers, health workers) to track a child's information. Children under 13 should not use the app themselves.
