# LaborLink

## Support

LaborLink connects customers with local workers and service providers: you can search for and book workers, chat with them, and view their profiles and ratings. Workers get a dashboard for managing job requests, bookings, verification status and their income/earnings history.

Need help, found a bug, or want your account and data deleted? Open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

<a id="privacy-policy"></a>
## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-25

### What we collect
- Account details: full name, email address, phone number, address, and your password (stored only as a salted hash).
- Worker profile (if you offer services): profession, rating, pricing, verification status, and the bio/portfolio photos you add to your public profile.
- Bookings and job details: service requested, worker, date, time, price, status, contact name and phone number, and job address.
- Chat messages and reviews you exchange with workers, and your saved/favourited workers.
- Worker earnings: job transaction history (service, customer, date, amount) shown on the worker income dashboard.

### Why we collect it
We use this data to run search and booking, connect you with workers, power in-app chat, show ratings/reviews, and calculate worker earnings.

### Storage and sharing
Your data is stored on the app server. We do not sell your data. We did not find any third-party analytics, payment or messaging services used in the app's current code.

### Retention and deletion
Your data is kept until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion, open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

### Children
Children under 13 should not use this app.
