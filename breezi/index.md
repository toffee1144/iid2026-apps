# Breezi

## Support

Breezi is an anxiety-relief app with breathing and grounding tools, mood tracking, a journal, sleep logs and calming affirmations. You can optionally connect to a counselor and share weekly summaries with them.

Need help, found a bug, or want your account and data deleted? Open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

### Medical disclaimer
Breezi is informational and self-help only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you are in crisis or in danger, contact your local emergency services or a qualified professional right away.

### What we collect
- Account details: email address, display name, and your password (stored only as a salted hash).
- Mood entries: the mood you pick, its intensity and any note you write.
- Journal entries: the text you write, its tag and the prompt used.
- Tool sessions: which relief exercise you used, how long, and your anxiety level before and after.
- Sleep logs: hours slept, sleep quality, date and any note.
- Favorite affirmations you save.
- Counselor connection: only if you choose to connect with a counselor using a join code, the connection and the weekly summaries you choose to share with them.

### Why we collect it
We use this data to run the features of the app: showing your history and trends, syncing your entries and, if you choose, sharing summaries with your counselor. This is health-related information, so please share only what you are comfortable with.

### Storage and sharing
Your data is stored on the app server. We do not sell your data. The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising or AI services, so no third party receives your data from the app. A counselor sees your information only if you connect to them.

### Retention and deletion
Your data is kept until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion, open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

### Children
Children under 13 should not use this app.
