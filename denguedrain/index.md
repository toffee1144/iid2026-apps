# DengueDrain

## Support

DengueDrain monitors drainage nodes to help find standing water that can become mosquito breeding sites. Inspectors see sensor readings, risk scores and alerts on a map and log field inspections.

Need help, found a bug, or want your account and data deleted? Open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

### Informational use
DengueDrain is informational and is not a substitute for professional medical or public health advice.

### What we collect
- Account details: name, email address, role (admin, inspector or viewer), and your password (stored only as a salted hash).
- Inspection reports: condition, notes, actions taken, captions, before and after photos (taken with the camera or chosen from your photo library) and GPS coordinates.
- Drainage node data: water level, temperature, humidity, battery, flow and presence readings, risk scores and alerts. This data comes from devices, not from people.

### Why we collect it
We use this data to run the app: sign-in, showing risk and alerts, and keeping a record of field inspections.

### Storage and sharing
Your data is stored on the app server. We do not sell your data. Third-party services receive only what they need: OpenAI may receive the latest risk level and sensor readings of a drainage node to write a short summary (no personal data), and OpenStreetMap receives map tile requests when you view the map.

### Retention and deletion
Your data is kept until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion, open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

### Children
Children under 13 should not use this app.
