# MoneyBuddy

## Support

MoneyBuddy is a kid-friendly savings app with a capybara mascot called Capy. Children track their allowance and spending, set savings goals, earn points and chat with Capy for spending tips.

Need help, found a bug, or want your account and data deleted? Open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

<a id="privacy-policy"></a>
## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

### What we collect
- Account details: name, email address, an optional PIN, and your password (both stored only as salted hashes).
- Money records you enter: balance, allowance, spending and saving transactions with categories, points history and shop purchases. These are in-app records only; the app does not connect to bank accounts or handle real payments.
- Savings goals: name, icon, target amount, amount saved and an optional photo taken with the camera or chosen from your photo library.
- Your mascot name and your chat messages with Capy.

### Why we collect it
We use this data to run the app: tracking savings, goals and points, and letting Capy give tips that fit your situation.

### Storage and sharing
Your data is stored on the app server. We do not sell your data. OpenAI receives your chat message, recent chat history and a short summary (balance, points and goal progress) so that Capy can reply.

### Retention and deletion
Your data is kept until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion, open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

### Children
MoneyBuddy is designed for children about 8 to 14 years old and should be used together with a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a child account and its data using the link above.
