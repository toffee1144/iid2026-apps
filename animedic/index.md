# Animedic

## Support

Animedic is a pet health app, with a focus on cat diseases. It offers an AI assistant, vet consultations and appointments, plasma donor matching, an emergency SOS to nearby vets and a community feed for pet owners and veterinarians.

Need help, found a bug, or want your account and data deleted? Open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

### Medical disclaimer
Animedic is informational only. The AI assistant and the app content are not a substitute for professional veterinary or medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, contact a veterinarian right away.

### What we collect
- Account details: name, email address, phone number, role (pet owner or vet), and your password (stored only as a salted hash).
- Pet profiles: name, species, breed, sex, age, weight, vaccination status, allergies, chronic conditions and last check-up date.
- AI chat messages you send to the assistant and its replies.
- Emergency SOS: your name, phone number, your pet name and condition, and your precise location, only when you send an SOS and allow location access.
- Plasma donor and request details, consultation messages, appointments, prescriptions and patient documents that owners and vets enter.
- Community posts, comments and likes.
- Push notification token for your device.

### Why we collect it
We use this data to run the app: consultations, matching plasma donors, sending SOS to nearby vets, answering your questions, keeping pet records and notifying you. Other users can see what you choose to post, and vets and owners see the details needed for a shared consultation or request.

### Storage and sharing
Your data is stored on the app server. We do not sell your data. Third-party services receive only what they need: OpenAI receives your chat messages to write the assistant replies, Firebase Cloud Messaging by Google delivers push notifications, and OpenStreetMap receives map tile requests when you view a map.

### Retention and deletion
Your data is kept until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion, open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

### Children
Children under 13 should not use this app.
