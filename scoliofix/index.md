# ScolioFix

## Support

ScolioFix is a photo-based posture screening app: you take 4 guided photos (front, back, side profile and a forward-bend shot) and the app analyzes them for possible signs of scoliosis, giving you a risk score, a dashboard and a history of past screenings.

Need help, found a bug, or want your account and data deleted? Open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-25

### Medical disclaimer
ScolioFix is a screening and awareness tool only. It is not a diagnosis and is not a substitute for an in-person evaluation by a doctor or other qualified health professional. If you have concerns about your spine or posture, please see a professional.

### What we collect
- Account details: full name, email address, date of birth, and your password (stored only as a salted hash).
- Screening photos: the 4 posture photos you take or choose from your photo library for each screening (front, back, side profile, forward-bend).
- Screening results: risk percentage and tier, per-photo findings, confidence level, the AI-generated analysis, and your screening history.
- Your reminder and health-tip preference settings. Posture reminders are scheduled on your own device only.

### Why we collect it
We use this data to run each screening, generate your risk analysis and recommendations, and show your dashboard and history.

### Storage and sharing
Your data and photos are stored on the app server. We do not sell your data. To generate the analysis, your screening photos are sent to OpenAI, which receives only what is needed to produce the report.

### Retention and deletion
Your data is kept until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion, open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

### Children
Children under 13 should not use this app.
