# I Need Help

## Support

I Need Help is a community assistance app that connects people who need help with nearby volunteers in their community. Seekers post a request, and helpers can accept it, chat and follow the request on a live map.

Need help, found a bug, or want your account and data deleted? Open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

<a id="privacy-policy"></a>
## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

### What we collect
- Account details: name, email address, phone number (if you provide one), a profile photo, and your password (stored only as a salted hash).
- Location: precise (GPS) and approximate location, used to match requests with nearby helpers and to show live tracking during an active request.
- Microphone and speech: used only for speech-to-text when you dictate a request or message. The app does not store the audio.
- Chat messages you send within a help request.
- Community details: the community you join or lead, and a community photo if a leader sets one from the photo library.
- Push notification token for your device.
- Usage data: streaks, completed-help counts, ratings given and received, coins and avatar or cosmetic settings.

### Why we collect it
We use this data to match seekers with nearby helpers, show live location during an active request, send notifications, keep chat history and calculate ratings, streaks and rewards. The other person on a request can see your name, profile photo, live location (during that request only) and chat messages.

### Storage and sharing
Your data is stored on the app server. We do not sell your data. Third-party services receive only what they need: Firebase Cloud Messaging by Google delivers push notifications, your device speech recognition service converts speech to text, and OpenStreetMap receives map tile requests when you view a map.

### Retention and deletion
Your data is kept until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion, open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

### Children
Children under 13 should not use this app.
