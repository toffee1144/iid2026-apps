# AeroTile

## Support

AeroTile is the companion app for the AeroTile smart biochar wall tile. It shows room comfort, how saturated each tile is, and alerts you when a tile needs to be regenerated.

Need help, found a bug, or want your account and data deleted? Open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

<a id="privacy-policy"></a>
## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

### What we collect
- The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, photos or contacts.
- Sensor data from your AeroTile device: room temperature and humidity, tile moisture and saturation, fan state and related readings, with recent history.
- Fan commands and regeneration actions that you send from the app to the device.
- App preferences such as language and room settings, which are stored on your own device only.

### Why we collect it
We use sensor data and commands to show your room comfort score, tile health and history, and to control the fan and regeneration flow.

### Storage and sharing
Sensor readings travel between your device, a message broker and the app server, and recent readings are kept on the server. We do not sell your data. The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising or sign-in services.

### Retention and deletion
Your data is kept until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion, open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

### Children
Children under 13 should not use this app.
