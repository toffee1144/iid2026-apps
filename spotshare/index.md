# SpotShare

## Support

SpotShare is a mobile platform for the spa and massage industry that brings spa owners, therapists and customers together. Spa owners manage rooms, supplies and memberships, therapists book rooms and serve clients, and customers book therapists.

Need help, found a bug, or want your account and data deleted? Open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

### What we collect
- Account details: email address, full name, your role (spa owner, therapist or customer), an optional profile photo, and your password (stored only as a salted hash).
- Spa listings you create: spa name, address, city, map coordinates, opening hours and photos.
- Therapist profiles: bio, experience, specializations, certifications, languages, services and prices.
- Bookings and activity: room and customer bookings, reviews and ratings, therapist notes about customers, supply usage, invoices, disputes, and payment records (method, amount and status). The app does not store card numbers.
- Messages you send to the other party of a booking.
- Photos you take with the camera or choose from your photo library to add pictures of a space.

### Why we collect it
We use this data only to run the features of the app: sign-in, listings, bookings, billing, reviews and messaging. Other users can see the profile, listing and review information that the app displays to them, and the person you book with can see your booking details and messages.

### Storage and sharing
Your data is stored on the app server. We do not sell your data. The app does not use third-party sign-in, analytics, advertising or AI services, so no third party receives your data from the app.

### Retention and deletion
Your data is kept until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion, open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

### Children
Children under 13 should not use this app.
