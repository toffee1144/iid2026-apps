# PCOScan

## Support

PCOScan is a PCOS self-screening and risk assessment companion. You answer a symptom questionnaire and can add symptom photos, and the app gives you a risk estimate with general suggestions.

Need help, found a bug, or want your account and data deleted? Open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

### Medical disclaimer
PCOScan is informational only. It is not a diagnosis and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Please talk to a qualified health professional about your symptoms.

### What we collect
- Account details: a username, your date of birth, and your password (stored only as a salted hash). No email address or real name is required.
- Questionnaire answers: age and age at first period, cycle length, period flow, duration and pain, weight and height, family history, and symptoms such as irregular periods, hair growth or thinning, and acne.
- Optional symptom photos (acne and skin patches) that you take with the camera or choose from your photo library.
- Screening results: scores, risk percentage, recommendations and the AI-generated analysis for each screening.
- Your settings for screening reminders, health tips and biometric login.

### Why we collect it
We use this data to calculate your screening result, generate the analysis report and show your screening history. This is health-related information, so please share only what you are comfortable with.

### Storage and sharing
Your data and photos are stored on the app server. We do not sell your data. To generate the analysis, your questionnaire answers and the photos you upload are sent to OpenAI, which receives only what is needed to produce the report.

### Retention and deletion
Your data is kept until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion, open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

### Children
Children under 13 should not use this app.
