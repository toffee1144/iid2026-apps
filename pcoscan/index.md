# Nurture Journal

## Support

Nurture Journal is a private cycle and symptom journal. You log short check-ins about your cycle, skin, energy and lifestyle, and look back at your history to notice patterns. It does not screen for, diagnose, treat or monitor any medical condition.

### Contact us

Questions, bug reports, feedback, or want your account and data deleted? Email us at [adityafarel840@gmail.com](mailto:adityafarel840@gmail.com) with "Nurture Journal" in the subject. We reply within 3 business days.

You can also open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

### Frequently asked questions

**Is this medical advice?** No. It is a personal journal only. Please talk to a qualified health professional about any health concern.

**I forgot my password.** Email us from the address you use and include your username, and we will help you regain access.

**How do I delete my account and data?** Email us with your username and the words "delete my account". We will remove your account and check-ins.

**Is my data shared?** No. Your check-ins are stored on our server and are not sold or shared with advertisers, AI services or other third parties. See the privacy policy below.

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

### Not medical advice
Nurture Journal is a personal journal. It is not a diagnosis tool and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

### What we collect
- Account details: a username, your date of birth, and your password (stored only as a salted hash). No email address or real name is required.
- Check-in answers that you choose to enter: age and age at first period, cycle length, period flow, duration and pain, weight and height, family history, and notes about skin and hair.
- Your settings for reminders, tips and biometric login.

The app does not collect photos, and it does not use your location, contacts or advertising ID.

### Why we collect it
We use this data to save your check-ins and show your history. This is health-related information, so please share only what you are comfortable with.

### Storage and sharing
Your data is stored on the app server. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with third parties or AI services.

### Retention and deletion
Your data is kept until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion, email us or open an issue at [https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues](https://github.com/toffee1144/iid2026-apps/issues) and include the app name.

### Children
Children under 13 should not use this app.
